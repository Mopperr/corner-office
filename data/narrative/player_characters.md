# Corner Office: Player Characters
Pick one at the start. Each one changes your starting space, money, items, and Marge's first email. All perks are small and cozy.

## Dana Reyes (`pc_dana`)
*Laid off on a Tuesday. Opened a business on a Wednesday.*

Dana spent nine years in accounts at Grayline Paper Co. until the whole floor got 'restructured.' She took her stapler, her mug, and a box of sticky notes home. Her garage is about to become headquarters.

- **Start:** Garage Startup (`office_garage_startup`): The standard starter office from the level specs.
- **Money:** $120
- **Items:** stapler_red, mug_worlds_okayest, Sticky Note Stack (new)
- **Perk: Spreadsheet Brain.** The Market chart shows a soft dotted line for yesterday's price. Just a handy hint.

**Email: Welcome to the garage, boss**

> Hey Dana,
> 
> Heard Grayline let you go. Their loss. My first office was a garage too. Smelled like diesel and big plans.
> 
> Unpack first. The whiteboard's yours to fill.
> 
> Keep the shiny side up,
> Mile Marker

## Hank "Gravel" Dubois (`pc_hank`)
*Thirty years of highway. Now he'd like a desk that doesn't move.*

Hank ran flatbed with Marge back in the CB days. He's off the road now, with a rented storage unit full of road souvenirs and no idea what to do next. Marge has some ideas.

- **Start:** Unit 14B Storage (`office_storage_unit`) NEW SPACE: NEW. Small 3x6 m storage unit with a roll-up door, one bare bulb, and a camp chair. Cozy once the string lights go up.
- **Money:** $100
- **Items:** Old CB Radio (new), Dented Thermos (new), string_lights
- **Perk: Road Legs.** Bumps on the drive shake the boxes a little less, about 1 point each.

**Email: Breaker breaker, Gravel**

> Gravel, you old so-and-so.
> 
> A storage unit, huh? I've parked in worse. Hang those lights and it's a palace.
> 
> Spare truck's yours whenever. You still know how to back up, I hope.
> 
> 10-4, good buddy,
> Mile Marker

## Milo Tran (`pc_milo`)
*Fresh diploma. Dorm room. Huge ambitions, tiny desk.*

Milo graduated in business last spring and still has one semester left on the dorm lease. His roommate gave him the corner by the window. It has a mini fridge and plenty of ambition.

- **Start:** Dorm Corner (`office_dorm_corner`) NEW SPACE: NEW. 3x3 m corner of a dorm room with a twin bed, window, and mini fridge. Roommate's half is visible but off-limits.
- **Money:** $90
- **Items:** beanbag_mustard, poster_motivational, Dorm Kettle (new)
- **Perk: Campus Buzz.** Market regulars send one extra friendly offer each in-game day.

**Email: Corner of a corner. I like it.**

> Hi Milo,
> 
> Starting from a dorm corner? Good. Small loads teach you to pack tight.
> 
> Eat something that isn't ramen. Then check the Market.
> 
> Shiny side up,
> Mile Marker

## Bea Lindqvist (`pc_bea`)
*Knits, glues, and sells it all from the kitchen table.*

Bea makes felt cacti and tiny knitted desk buddies and sells them online. Orders keep growing, and so does the yarn pile on the kitchen table. Her cat thinks it's all for him.

- **Start:** Kitchen Table HQ (`office_kitchen_table`) NEW SPACE: NEW. Cozy 4x4 m kitchen nook with a round table, a window over the sink, and yarn baskets. Good early shop-front seed.
- **Money:** $110
- **Items:** plant_cactus, kettle, Yarn Basket (new)
- **Perk: Handmade Touch.** Repair taps on damaged items work in one tap instead of two.

**Email: Kitchen tables count**

> Hi Bea,
> 
> My first logbook was written at a kitchen table. Coffee rings and all.
> 
> Your felt cacti would sell great at a truck stop. I'd buy two.
> 
> 10-4,
> Mile Marker

## Sol Achebe (`pc_sol`)
*Freelancer, laptop, and a standing order for one more refill.*

Sol designs logos from the corner table at Whisker & Bean. Priya never charges for refills. Biscuit sometimes sleeps on the keyboard, which Sol counts as feedback.

- **Start:** Cafe Corner (`office_cafe_corner`) NEW SPACE: NEW. One corner table inside Whisker & Bean (about 3x3 m), with a window, a wall shelf, and ambient cafe regulars. Could reuse the Cat Cafe set dressing and pink palette.
- **Money:** $130
- **Items:** lamp_moody, plant_fern, cat_bed
- **Perk: Cafe Regular.** Priya's Cat Cafe job is offered from the start, and Biscuit always leaves a pawprint photo.

**Email: Priya says you're good people**

> Hi Sol,
> 
> Priya says you've held that corner table for two years. That's a long haul.
> 
> When you want a bigger desk, I've got a truck. Biscuit rides free.
> 
> Over and out,
> Mile Marker
