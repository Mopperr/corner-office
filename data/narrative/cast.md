# Corner Office: Cast
Tone: warm sitcom office. Nobody's mean, nothing's urgent.

## Player
**Dana Reyes**, a laid-off office worker. She starts in the Garage Startup with $120. This slice plays as Dana only.

## Mentor
**Marge "Mile Marker" Holloway** (CB handle: Mile Marker). She drove long-haul for 30 years, then started Corner Movers out of her garage. Now semi-retired, she mentors you through emails and job briefs. She keeps a hours-of-service logbook for everything, naps included, and she's very particular about load straps. She rates every truck-stop coffee she's ever had. Signs off with "10-4," "Keep the shiny side up," or "Mile Marker, over and out."

## Recurring Clients (each has a small story arc across 10 jobs)
- **Priya Okafor**, owner of the Whisker & Bean cat cafe, and her cat **Biscuit**, who hides in a box on every job. *Arc:* cafe move, then a pop-up stand, then a second location (jobs 1, 5, 9).
- **Delbert Finch**, retired clockmaker with 41 named clocks. *Arc:* he downsizes, takes up a hobby, and moves in with someone new (jobs 2, 6, 10).
- **Juno Park**, drummer in a band called The Laundromats. Always late, always cheerful. *Arc:* new flat, then a practice space, then the band's first tour van (jobs 3, 7).
- **Rosa & Ray Pemberton**, twin florists who disagree on everything. *Arc:* they merge their shops, then fully make up (jobs 4, 8).

## Market Regulars
- **StaplerSteve88**, collector who pays well for any stapler with "character."
- **Lamp_Lady**, sells desk lamps and calls every one of them "moody."
- **KeyClackr**, reviews every keyboard by its clack.
