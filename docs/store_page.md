# Steam Short Description (300 characters or fewer)

**Main:**
Pack up your old office and make a new one feel like home. Trade quirky office goods on a retro computer, or take cozy mover jobs around town. Pack boxes, drive the Corner Movers truck through the rain, and unpack. Grow from a Garage Startup to a real corner office. No rush, no fail.

**Alt A:**
A cozy game about moving offices. Rescue a cat from a box, help a clockmaker move 41 clocks, and flip vintage staplers on a beige CRT. Decorate your way from a garage to the corner office, one box at a time.

**Alt B:**
Unpack, decorate, repeat. Earn money by trading odd office treasures or working mover jobs for lovable regulars, then spend it on plants, lamps, and bigger offices. Warm, gently funny, and impossible to fail.

**Alt C:**
Your office fits in nine boxes. Maybe ten. Trade, haul, and decorate your way from a Garage Startup to the Corner Office in this low-stakes cozy game about moving, mugs, and the cat who's always in the last box.
