# What Dana is earning for

The ladder on the computer's **Life** page (`data/life.json`, `scripts/life.gd`).
Rungs cost money and may need a Rep rank, a shop tier or an earlier rung.
Each one pays back in play. The first million is a milestone, not a
purchase, and it is a long way off by design: the early rungs come in the
first hours, the trucks and counters across many in-game weeks.

## Every night

Each extra truck brings in $140 (more with Rep), each extra counter
brings in 50% of what the yard took that day (at least $40 counts),
and Pickle eats $4. The night card lists it all.

## Milestones

- **The first million** at $1,000,000 earned: A million dollars earned, one box at a time. Marge writes.

After the million the late game opens (see the road to a billion, coming).
